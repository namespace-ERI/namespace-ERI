I am a second-year PhD student at the [Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence](http://ai.ruc.edu.cn/), [Renmin University of China](https://www.ruc.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Zhicheng Dou](http://playbigdata.ruc.edu.cn/dou/).

I am currently a research intern at the **LLM Center, Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory**, working on **ML Coding** with [Prof. Tao Gui](https://guitaowufeng.github.io/). Previously, I was a research intern at the **Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI)**, focusing on agent memory and autonomous research.

### Research Interests

- **Long-Horizon Agents:** self-evolving agents, collective reasoning, and agent harnesses;
- **Agent Memory:** structured memory, retrieval, and test-time learning;
- **ML Coding & AutoResearch:** autonomous research agents and reusable operational knowledge.
